## When to use this

* You are picking Solo, Teams, or Teams Pro for the first time
* You need signing order, minimum signatures, or multi-recipient payouts
* You want gated file packets or payout packets as a solo operator
* You want long-term retention without buying the highest team tier
* You are unsure whether payout packets need extra approval beyond the plan

## How it works

| You are… | Start here |
|----------|------------|
| One person who needs handoffs and proof exports | **Solo** |
| A team sharing drafts and templates | **Teams** |
| Need signing order, minimum signatures, conditional file release, or complex payouts | **Teams Pro** |
| Large org with procurement | **Enterprise** - [contact sales](mailto:sales@filosign.xyz) |

### Sending limits

| Plan | Documents per month | Signers per document |
|------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Solo** | 10 | Up to 3 |
| **Teams** | 15 per seat (shared pool) | Up to 10 |
| **Teams Pro** | 25 per seat (shared pool) | Up to 15 |

Quotas reset each calendar month.

### What every paid plan includes

* **Private documents** (encrypted before upload)
* **Proof exports** (proof report and proof packet)
* **Gated file packets** and **basic USDC payout packets** (payouts need workspace [payout access](/docs/workspace/payout-access/) approval)
* Ability to add **archival storage** separately for multi-year retention

### Solo handoffs (Solo and above)

* Gated file packets on sends
* Single-leg USDC payout packets when signing finishes
* Draft review links for external clients

### Team features (Teams and above)

* Shared templates and team drafts
* See teammates' envelopes on **Home**
* Comments on drafts and documents
* Per-packet recipient selection for gated files
* Pooled document quota that grows with seats

### Teams Pro extras

* Signers sign in **order** you set
* **Minimum signatures** (not everyone required)
* Multi-recipient payout rules
* Signature-conditional release for gated files

## Example workflow

A freelancer on **Solo** sends contracts with gated deliverables and payout packets, then exports proof packets for their records. When two colleagues join, they upgrade to **Teams** for shared drafts and templates. They add archival separately for three-year retention without needing Teams Pro until they need signing order or conditional file release.

## Solo operator FAQ

**I work alone but need payouts or gated files.** Solo includes basic handoffs. Subscribe to Solo, then request [payout access](/docs/workspace/payout-access/) in Workspace settings if you plan to attach USDC payouts.

**I do not want team features.** Stay on Solo for handoffs without shared templates or team visibility. Teams at **one seat** is available if you want pooled quota or per-packet recipient selection without inviting colleagues.

**When is Teams Pro worth it?** When you need sequential signing, quorum, multi-recipient payout rules, or signature-conditional file release.

## What to watch out for

* Long-term retention is a **yearly archival subscription** in Workspace settings. You do **not** need Teams Pro just for storage.
* USDC payout packets require workspace [payout access](/docs/workspace/payout-access/) approval from Filosign, plus normal wallet setup.
* Some Teams Pro items on the pricing page are still on the [roadmap](/docs/plans/roadmap/).

## How to change plans in the app

1. Open **Workspace settings** → **Billing**.
2. Start a trial, subscribe, or switch between Teams and Teams Pro.
3. Open **Archival** separately if you need multi-year retention.
4. Open **Payout attachment access** before attaching payout packets.
