## When do I need this?

Download a **proof packet** when you need to:

* Send a closed deal to **legal** or **finance**
* Keep a **copy that does not depend** on Filosign years later
* Show **who signed, when, and which fields** were completed
* Attach proof to an **audit** or **dispute** file
* Record whether payout packets or gated files were part of the workflow.

Traditional e-sign tools give you a signed PDF. Filosign gives you that plus a structured proof report and optional one-file merge.

## When can I download it?

After the envelope is **fully signed**, or **voided** before everyone finished. While people are still signing, exports stay off.

## How to download

On the document page, use the header buttons:

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Package icon** | Full ZIP (best for archives and counsel) |
| **Scroll icon** | Proof report only (PDF) |
| **Download icon** | Document with proof section appended |

## What's in the ZIP

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Original documents** | The signed files, readable on any computer |
| **Proof report** | Readable summary: parties, dates, fields, payouts, and file releases when applicable |
| **Document + proof PDF** | One PDF to email stakeholders |
| **README** | Short index of IDs and how an expert can verify details |

You do not need to read the README for everyday use. Counsel or IT may use it for deeper checks.

## Who should save a copy

* **Senders** when the deal closes
* **Signers** for personal records
* **Anyone** who might ask "prove this was signed" later

Even if you buy [archival storage](/docs/storage/how-retention-works/), keep your own packet for critical agreements.
