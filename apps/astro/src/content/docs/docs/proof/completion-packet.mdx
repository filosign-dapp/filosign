title: Proof packets

A proof packet is the record you keep when someone later asks what happened: who signed, when, which fields were completed, and whether payout packets or gated files were part of the workflow. Download it after the envelope is fully signed or voided.

## When to use this

* Send a closed deal to **legal** or **finance**
* Keep a **copy that does not depend** on Filosign years later
* Show **who signed, when, and which fields** were completed
* Attach proof to an **audit** or **dispute** file
* Record whether payout packets or gated files were part of the workflow

## How it works

1. Signers complete the envelope (or the sender voids it before everyone finishes).
2. Filosign unlocks proof downloads on the document page.
3. You choose a download format: full ZIP, proof report only, or document with proof appended.
4. The ZIP bundles signed documents, a readable proof report, optional merged PDF, and a short README for technical reviewers.

While people are still signing, exports stay off.

## Example workflow

A founder closes an NDA with an investor. After both parties sign, the founder downloads the proof packet ZIP and saves it to the company's data room. Months later, finance asks who signed and when; the proof report inside the ZIP answers without logging back into Filosign.

## What to watch out for

* Proof downloads unlock only after the envelope is **fully signed** or **voided** before completion.
* Even if you buy [archival storage](/docs/storage/how-retention-works/), keep your own packet for critical agreements.
* The README inside the ZIP is for counsel or IT; everyday readers can use the proof report PDF.
* Filosign does not decide whether a document is legally valid in your jurisdiction. See [E-signature evidence](/docs/proof/e-signature-evidence/).

## How to do it in the app

On the document page, use the header buttons:

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Package icon** | Full ZIP (best for archives and counsel) |
| **Scroll icon** | Proof report only (PDF) |
| **Download icon** | Document with proof section appended |

### What's in the ZIP

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Original documents** | The signed files, readable on any computer |
| **Proof report** | Readable summary: parties, dates, fields, payouts, and file releases when applicable |
| **Document + proof PDF** | One PDF to email stakeholders |
| **README** | Short index of IDs and how an expert can verify details |

### Who should save a copy

* **Senders** when the deal closes
* **Signers** for personal records
* **Anyone** who might ask "prove this was signed" later
