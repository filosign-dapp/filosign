---
title: How to read the proof report
description: Understand the proof report PDF without technical background.
---

The **proof report** is a completion certificate for one signing workflow: who participated, what was signed, when signatures were recorded, and whether payout or file-release events were attached. Download it alone or inside the [proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/).

## When to use this

* Finance or legal needs a quick review without opening the signed contract PDF
* You want to confirm parties, timestamps, and field completions before archiving
* You are deciding which download format to email (report only vs full ZIP vs merged PDF)
* An auditor asks for a human-readable summary of the workflow

## How it works

The proof report summarizes one envelope in plain language:

1. **About this record** and quick summary on the cover
2. **Who signed** and **parties**
3. **Signing timeline**
4. **Field placements**: signature, date, and text fields completed
5. Optional sections for payout packets and gated files
6. **How to verify independently** with a link to the public verifier

Most readers only need the first five sections. Cryptographic and chain details live in the proof packet ZIP and are checked through the [independent verifier](https://verify.filosign.xyz), not in the PDF body.

## Example workflow

A bookkeeper receives a signed services agreement. They download the proof report PDF only (scroll icon on the document page), confirm the vendor and dates match expectations, and file it alongside the invoice without parsing the contract itself.

## What to watch out for

* Use the [independent verifier](https://verify.filosign.xyz) when you need cryptographic or on-chain checks. Drop the proof packet ZIP on the page; you do not need to read `_proofs/` files yourself.
* Payout and gated-file sections appear only when the sender added those features.
* The proof report is evidence, not a legal opinion on enforceability. See [E-signature evidence](/docs/proof/e-signature-evidence/).

Traditional e-sign tools often attach a certificate PDF to the signed file. Filosign separates the **signed document** and a **detailed report** so finance and legal can review evidence without parsing the contract file itself.

## How to download the right format

| Need | Download |
|------|----------|
| Email one file to a client | Document with proof PDF |
| Internal legal review | Proof report only |
| Full archive | Proof packet ZIP |

On the document page:

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Package icon** | Full ZIP |
| **Scroll icon** | Proof report only (PDF) |
| **Download icon** | Document with proof section appended |
