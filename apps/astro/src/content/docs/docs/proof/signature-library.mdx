---
title: Signature library
description: Save your signature and initial once, reuse them when signing.
---

If you sign often, drawing your signature every time is tedious. The **signature library** stores styles you reuse across envelopes, similar to saving a signature in other e-sign apps.

## When to use this

* You sign multiple envelopes per week
* You want default signature and initial styles for auto-fill
* You are setting up your profile after onboarding
* You wonder whether changing your library affects old signed documents

## How it works

1. Create a **signature** and/or **initial** (draw, type, or upload).
2. Mark one of each as **default** if you want auto-fill.
3. When you sign, Filosign can fill signature and initial fields from your defaults.
4. When you finish signing an envelope, Filosign saves a **snapshot** for that document.

Updating your library later does **not** alter agreements you already signed. Your [proof export](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) shows what the signature looked like at signing time.

Name, date, and email fields may auto-fill from your profile when the sender assigned those field types to you.

## Example workflow

A contractor saves a drawn signature and typed initial as defaults. Each new SOW takes one click to fill signature fields instead of redrawing every time.

## What to watch out for

* You can change the style before you submit on any given envelope.
* Library changes do not retroactively update completed documents.
* Signature images in proof exports reflect what you used at signing time.

## How to set up your library in the app

1. Open **Profile** → **Signature library** (or finish onboarding).
2. Create a **signature** and/or **initial** (draw, type, or upload).
3. Mark one of each as **default** if you want auto-fill.
