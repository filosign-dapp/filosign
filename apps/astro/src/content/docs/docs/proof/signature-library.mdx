## Why save signatures?

If you sign often, drawing your signature every time is tedious. The **signature library** stores styles you reuse across envelopes, similar to saving a signature in other e-sign apps.

## Set up your library

1. Open **Profile** → **Signature library** (or finish onboarding).
2. Create a **signature** and/or **initial** (draw, type, or upload).
3. Mark one of each as **default** if you want auto-fill.

## When you sign

Filosign can fill signature and initial fields from your defaults. You can change the style before you submit.

Name, date, and email fields may auto-fill from your profile when the sender assigned those field types to you.

## Will changing my signature change old documents?

**No.** When you finish signing an envelope, Filosign saves a **snapshot** for that document. Updating your library later does not alter agreements you already signed.

Your [proof export](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) shows what the signature looked like at signing time.
