## Can Filosign read my documents?

**No, not in normal use.** Your files are locked **before they leave your browser**. Filosign stores locked copies and the keys that let invited people unlock them. Filosign does not hold the master key that reveals document text.

That is a meaningful difference from many traditional e-sign tools, where the provider often holds readable copies on their servers.

## Who can open a document?

Only people you put on the envelope:

- The **sender**
- **Signers** and **viewers** you listed
- **Teammates** in a shared workspace (when the envelope belongs to a team)
- **Draft reviewers** if you shared a draft link before send

If someone is not invited, they cannot read the file, even if they work at Filosign.

## What about passwords and recovery?

You sign in and confirm actions in the app (including with a linked wallet). That unlocks your personal keys for the session.

If you set up a **recovery phrase** during onboarding, guard it like a password. Filosign cannot reset it for you.

Some recipients get a **one-time phrase** with their invite if they do not have an account yet. They need that phrase to open the document the first time.

## Drafts vs sent envelopes

| | Draft | Sent envelope |
|---|-------|---------------|
| **When** | Before you hit Send | After Send |
| **Purpose** | Work in progress | Completed workflow with proof |
| **Who sees it** | You and people you share the draft with | Sender, signers, viewers on the roster |

Sending turns your draft into a tracked envelope with signing records and exportable proof.

## How does this compare to DocuSign?

Both tools help people sign electronically. Filosign emphasizes **privacy by default**: the document text is not meant to be readable on Filosign's servers. You still get signing records, timestamps, and exports for disputes or audits.
