---
title: Verification records
description: What happens if someone disputes who signed or when, and how signing events are recorded.
---

Filosign records signing events in a way that can be checked outside the app. This helps your proof packet remain useful even when the document needs to be reviewed later by finance, legal, a grant reviewer, or a counterparty.

Everyday users can rely on the proof packet. Technical reviewers can inspect the underlying public references when deeper verification is needed.

## What details are recorded?

Filosign builds a **verification record** when you run a workflow. It captures:

* **Who** sent it and **who** signed (names, emails, and account ties in your proof export).
* **When** each person signed and opened the document.
* **Which fields** each signer completed.
* Whether the envelope was **voided** or a signer was **changed** before completion.
* Payout or gated-file events when those were part of the workflow.

You download this as a **proof report** or **proof packet**. See [Proof packets](/docs/proof/completion-packet/).

## How records are verified

The signing record is designed to be tamper-evident and verifiable outside the app. Filosign anchors key facts to a public record that third parties can check. That supports "this agreement really was signed on this date by these people."

Some details (the actual PDF bytes, signature images, typed field values) stay in **encrypted storage and exports**, not in that public record.

## Can Filosign delete a signature from history?

Filosign can **void** an incomplete envelope (cancel the workflow). That adds a void entry. It does **not** erase signatures that already happened. Plan for that if wallets or emails are sensitive.

## Do signers need crypto knowledge?

**No.** Filosign handles the technical steps so signers use a normal browser flow. Authorizing a signature feels like confirming an action in the app, not managing cryptocurrency.

## How is this different from a DocuSign certificate?

Traditional tools attach a certificate PDF to the signed file. Filosign gives you:

* A **human-readable proof report**
* A **proof packet** with documents plus evidence
* Independent references auditors can verify outside Filosign

Filosign does **not** tell you whether a document is legally valid in your country. See [E-signature evidence](/docs/proof/e-signature-evidence/).
