---
title: Download your own copy
description: Why you should save a proof packet even when Filosign stores documents long term.
---

**Yes, for important agreements.** Filosign can store encrypted copies and offer archival plans, but you should still keep your own **proof packet** for anything that might go to legal, finance, or an audit later.

Think of it like email: the provider keeps a copy, but you still save the contract PDF that matters.

## When to use this

* A deal just closed and you want a copy that does not depend on Filosign years later
* Compliance wants a local archive on your drive or data room
* You are about to cancel a plan or let archival renewal lapse
* Counsel asked for "everything in one place" without app login access

## How it works

When the envelope is **fully signed** (or **voided** before completion), open the document and download the proof packet (ZIP). It includes:

* The signed document files
* A readable proof report (who signed, when, which fields were completed)
* A merged PDF if you want one file to share

Filosign keeps a fast copy for everyday access and backs up encrypted copies to long-term storage in parallel. Downloading a proof packet gives **you** a copy that does not depend on subscriptions, retention terms, or account access years later.

## Example workflow

A founder closes an investor SAFE. The same day, they download the proof packet ZIP to the company data room and email the proof report PDF to finance. Six months later, they still have the files even if nobody logs into Filosign.

## What to watch out for

* Best time to download is **as soon as the deal is done**, while everyone still has access in the app.
* If you cancel your plan, you get a **grace period** to export important envelopes before long-term copies are scheduled for removal.
* Your own download stays with you even if archival renewal lapses.

## How to download in the app

1. Open the completed or voided envelope.
2. Click the **Package icon** in the header for the full ZIP.
3. Optionally download the proof report only (scroll icon) or document with proof appended (download icon).

See [What's in the proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/).

### Who should download

| Role | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Sender** | Your deal file and proof for internal records |
| **Signer** | Personal copy with evidence you signed |
| **Counsel or finance** | One packet to review without logging into Filosign |
