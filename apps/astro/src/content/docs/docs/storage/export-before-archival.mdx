## Should I download anything myself?

**Yes, for important agreements.** Filosign can store encrypted copies and offer archival plans, but you should still keep your own **completion packet** for anything that might go to legal, finance, or an audit later.

Think of it like email: the provider keeps a copy, but you still save the contract PDF that matters.

## What to download

When the envelope is **fully signed** (or **voided** before completion), open the document and download the **completion packet** (ZIP). It includes:

- The signed document files
- A readable proof report (who signed, when, which fields were completed)
- A merged PDF if you want one file to share

See [What's in the completion packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/).

## When to do it

Best time: **as soon as the deal is done**, while everyone still has access in the app.

Filosign may move encrypted copies into long-term storage after about **30 days**. Downloading early does not stop that process. It gives **you** a copy that does not depend on subscriptions, retention terms, or account access years later.

## Who should download

| Role | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Sender** | Your deal file and proof for internal records |
| **Signer** | Personal copy with evidence you signed |
| **Counsel or finance** | One packet to review without logging into Filosign |

## If you cancel your plan

You get a **grace period** to export important envelopes before long-term copies are scheduled for removal. If you use **archival**, renewal terms control how long Filosign keeps workspace documents. Your own download stays with you either way.
