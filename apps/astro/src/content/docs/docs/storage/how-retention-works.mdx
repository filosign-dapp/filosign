After everyone signs, Filosign keeps an **encrypted copy** of the envelope so you can open it again in the app. Paid workspaces also get backup retention tied to the subscription. If you need **years** of extended retention, buy a separate **archival plan** (subject to plan terms and the Terms of Service).

## When to use this

* Compliance or record-keeping requires keeping signed agreements for years
* You want Filosign to hold encrypted copies for a fixed term without relying only on your own downloads
* You are choosing between Solo, Teams, and a separate archival subscription
* You need to understand how long-term backup relates to your workspace plan

## How it works

1. The signed envelope stays available in your dashboard right away.
2. Filosign keeps a fast copy on everyday cloud storage so you can open documents normally in the app.
3. In parallel, Filosign backs up encrypted copies to **Filecoin Onchain Cloud** for long-term retention (independent of how fast you open the file day to day).
4. If you buy **archival**, Filosign extends how long those backups are funded on Filecoin for the term you chose (1 or 3 years).

You and your recipients still open documents the same way in the app. Nothing changes in day-to-day use.

### Archival storage (separate subscription)

**Archival** is a **yearly subscription** you add in **Workspace settings**. It is separate from whether you use Solo, Teams, or Teams Pro for sending documents. You **do not need Teams Pro** to buy archival.

| Plan | Keeps documents for |
|------|---------------------|
| Yearly | 1 year (renews each year) |
| 3-year | 3 years |

Archival applies to **all signed documents in that workspace** for the active term.

Today, archival is purchased from a Filosign **workspace** with a paid signing plan (Solo or Teams). Archival is an **add-on** for retention, not a replacement for the app itself.

## Example workflow

A small law firm on Solo sends NDAs monthly but must keep signed copies for three years. They subscribe to Solo for sending and add a **3-year archival** plan in Workspace settings. After each deal closes, they also download a [proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) for their own drive.

## What to watch out for

* Filosign cannot keep documents forever without an active plan or archival subscription.
* Filosign cannot recover plaintext if everyone loses access and nobody exported a copy.
* Archival is about **keeping what you already signed**. It is not a substitute for downloading your own proof packet.
* Traditional e-sign tools also store completed envelopes for a period tied to your subscription. Filosign adds optional multi-year archival, downloadable proof packets, and private encryption.

## How to manage retention in the app

1. Open **Workspace settings** → **Archival** to buy or renew a retention plan.
2. After signing finishes, download a [proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) for your own records.
3. Open a completed envelope to see **storage** or **retention** status. See [Storage status in the app](/docs/storage/status-in-app/).
