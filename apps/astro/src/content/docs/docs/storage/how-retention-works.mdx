## Will I still have my documents years from now?

That depends on your plan and whether you add **archival storage**.

After everyone signs, Filosign keeps an **encrypted copy** of the envelope so you can open it again in the app. Paid workspaces also get backup retention tied to the subscription. If you need **years** of guaranteed storage, buy a separate **archival plan**.

## What happens after signing

1. The signed envelope stays available in your dashboard right away.
2. For about **30 days**, Filosign keeps a fast, easy-to-reach copy while you finish downloads or internal review.
3. After that, encrypted copies move into long-term backup storage for paid workspaces.
4. If you buy **archival**, Filosign keeps documents for the term you chose (1, 3, or 5 years).

You and your recipients still open documents the same way in the app. Nothing changes in day-to-day use.

## Archival storage (separate subscription)

**Archival** is a **yearly subscription** you add in **Workspace settings**. It is separate from whether you use Solo, Teams, or Teams Pro for sending documents.

Choose archival when:

- Compliance or record-keeping requires keeping signed agreements for years
- You want Filosign to hold encrypted copies for a fixed term without relying only on your own downloads
- You care about long-term retention more than advanced features like signing order or multi-party payouts

You **do not need Teams Pro** to buy archival. Open **Workspace settings** → **Archival** and pick a term:

| Plan | Keeps documents for |
|------|---------------------|
| Yearly | 1 year (renews each year) |
| 3-year | 3 years |
| 5-year | 5 years |

Archival applies to **all signed documents in that workspace** for the active term.

Archival is about **keeping what you already signed**. It is not a substitute for downloading your own proof packet if you want a copy on your laptop or shared drive. See [Download your own copy](/docs/storage/export-before-archival/).

## Do I still need a signing plan?

Today, archival is purchased from a Filosign **workspace** with a paid signing plan (Solo or Teams). Sending and signing use that main subscription. Archival is an **add-on** for retention, not a replacement for the app itself.

If you only need a few sends per month plus long retention, **Solo plus archival** may be enough. You do not need the highest team tier for storage alone.

## What Filosign cannot promise

- Keeping documents forever without an active plan or archival subscription
- Recovering plaintext if everyone loses access and nobody exported a copy
- Replacing your own backups for critical deals

## How this compares to traditional e-sign tools

Tools like DocuSign also store completed envelopes for a period tied to your subscription. Filosign adds:

- **Optional multi-year archival** as its own subscription
- **Proof packets** you can download and keep anywhere
- **Private encryption** so Filosign does not read document contents in normal operation

See [Document privacy](/docs/security/encrypted-workflows/) and [Proof for legal review](/docs/proof/completion-packet/).

## Behind the scenes (optional)

Permanent archival storage is powered by **Filecoin Onchain Cloud**. In plain terms: Filosign stores your encrypted files with a **verifiable promise** to keep them for the term you paid for, checked by independent storage deals, not only on Filosign's everyday servers. You do not need to understand the technology to use it. The app shows retention status on each document.
