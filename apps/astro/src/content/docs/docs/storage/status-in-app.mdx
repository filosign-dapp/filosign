## Where do I see this?

Open a **completed** envelope from **Home**. The document view may show a short **storage** or **retention** status.

Use it to answer: "Is Filosign still holding this document, and for how long?"

## What the labels mean

| What you see | In plain terms |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Available** | You can open the document normally in the app |
| **Backing up** | Filosign is moving an encrypted copy into long-term storage |
| **Archived** | An archival plan is keeping this document for the term you bought |
| **Ending soon** | Retention or your plan is about to expire; download a copy if you need one |

Exact wording can vary slightly. The important part is whether you still have access and whether an archival term applies.

## Something looks wrong?

1. Confirm signing is **finished** or the envelope was **voided** (exports and status apply to closed workflows).
2. Check **Workspace settings** → **Billing** if your main plan is still active.
3. Check **Archival** if you bought a retention plan.
4. Download a [completion packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) for your own records.

Contact support if a completed document shows **backing up** for many weeks with no change.
