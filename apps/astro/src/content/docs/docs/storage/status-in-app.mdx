---
title: Storage status in the app
description: What the retention labels on a signed document mean.
---

Open a **completed** envelope from **Home**. The document view may show a short **storage** or **retention** status. Use it to answer: "Is Filosign still holding this document, and for how long?"

## When to use this

* You want to confirm Filosign is still storing a signed agreement
* An archival plan is ending soon and you need to download a copy
* A document shows **backing up** for a long time and you are unsure what that means
* Finance asks whether retention is active on a closed deal

## How it works

| What you see | In plain terms |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Available** | You can open the document normally in the app |
| **Backing up** | Filosign is moving an encrypted copy into long-term storage |
| **Archived** | An archival plan is keeping this document for the term you bought |
| **Ending soon** | Retention or your plan is about to expire; download a copy if you need one |

Exact wording can vary slightly. The important part is whether you still have access and whether an archival term applies.

## Example workflow

A compliance lead opens a two-year-old NDA and sees **Archived** with a 3-year term. They confirm the workspace archival subscription is active and download a fresh [proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) for an external audit file.

## What to watch out for

* Status labels apply to **finished** or **voided** workflows, not in-progress envelopes.
* **Ending soon** means you should download your own copy if you need one after retention lapses.
* Contact support if a completed document shows **backing up** for many weeks with no change.

## How to check status in the app

1. Open a completed envelope from **Home**.
2. Read the storage or retention label on the document view.
3. Open **Workspace settings** → **Billing** if your main plan might be inactive.
4. Open **Workspace settings** → **Archival** if you bought a retention plan.
5. Download a [proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) for your own records.
