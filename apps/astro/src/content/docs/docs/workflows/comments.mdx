title: Comments on drafts and envelopes
description: Encrypted team comments while composing drafts and on sent envelopes.

**Comments** let workspace members coordinate on drafts and open envelopes without leaving Filosign. Comments are **end-to-end encrypted** like the documents they discuss.

## When to use this

* Legal or finance wants internal notes on a draft before send
* Your team needs to agree on a [void](/docs/workflows/envelope-governance/) or [signer amendment](/docs/workflows/envelope-governance/) before acting
* You want to flag payout or attachment questions on an open envelope

## How it works

Comments on **drafts** and **documents** require **Teams** or **Teams Pro**.

**Draft comments** appear on saved team drafts while you are still composing. Everyone who can access the draft can read and post.

**Envelope comments** appear on the sign or document page header. Everyone who can decrypt the envelope can read and post.

Comments are not included as formal proof fields in the proof report. Workflow events and signatures are.

## Example workflow

Before voiding a sent agreement, an admin posts in envelope comments: "Legal approved void; waiting for finance." The team aligns in the encrypted thread, then the admin voids from the document page.

## What to watch out for

* Comments are not public or visible to recipients outside the encrypted thread.
* Comments are not a substitute for legal advice or external counsel review.
* Comments do not replace [draft review links](/docs/workflows/drafts/) when external clients need view-only access.

## How to comment in the app

### On a draft

1. Open a saved team draft on the add-sign step.
2. Open the **Comments** panel.
3. Post notes visible to everyone who can access the draft.

### On a sent envelope

1. Open the sign or document page.
2. Open **Comments** in the header.
3. Post and read notes with teammates who can decrypt the envelope.
