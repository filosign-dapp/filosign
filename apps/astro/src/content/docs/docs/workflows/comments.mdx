---
title: Comments on drafts and envelopes
description: Encrypted team comments while composing drafts and on sent envelopes.
---

**Comments** let workspace members coordinate on drafts and open envelopes without leaving Filosign. Comments are **end-to-end encrypted** like the documents they discuss.

## Plans

Comments on **drafts** and **documents** require **Teams** or **Teams Pro**.

## Draft comments

While composing a saved team draft:

1. Open the **Comments** panel on the add-sign step.
2. Post notes visible to everyone who can access the draft.

Use draft comments for internal review ("Legal wants clause 4 moved", "Wait for finance before send").

Draft comments require **Teams+** and a saved team draft.

## Envelope comments

On the sign or document page, open **Comments** in the header.

Everyone who can decrypt the envelope can read and post. Typical uses:

* Coordinate a [signer amendment](/docs/workflows/envelope-governance/)
* Discuss a [void](/docs/workflows/envelope-governance/) before executing
* Flag payout or attachment questions

Comments are not a substitute for legal advice or external counsel review.

## What comments are not

* Public or visible to recipients outside the encrypted thread
* Included as formal proof fields in the proof report (workflow events and signatures are)
* A replacement for [draft review links](/docs/workflows/drafts/) when external clients need view-only access
