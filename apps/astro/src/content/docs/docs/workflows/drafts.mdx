**Drafts** let you save an envelope before you send it. Come back later, collaborate with your team, share a view-only copy with external reviewers, and send when you are ready.

## When to use this

* You are mid-compose and need to pause before send
* Teammates should review recipients, fields, or payouts before the envelope goes out
* External counsel needs a view-only copy without signing access
* You want browser autosave plus a workspace-backed saved draft

## How it works

### While you are editing (browser)

On **New envelope**, Filosign keeps your progress in the browser: recipients, documents, signature fields, payouts, and extra files. You can refresh the page in the same browser without losing that work.

### Saved to your workspace

When you click **Save draft**, Filosign encrypts everything and stores it in your workspace. Teammates with access can open it from **Drafts** in the sidebar. A saved draft includes recipients, message, field placement, payout settings, and file bundles.

**Share draft** emails external reviewers a view-only link. They can read the document but cannot sign or pay until you send the envelope.

## Example workflow

A finance team prepares a vendor agreement with payout and gated-file settings. They save the draft, leave comments for legal ("wait for clause 4 approval"), share a read-only link with outside counsel, then send once everyone agrees.

## What to watch out for

* If two people edit a saved draft at once, Filosign warns you instead of silently overwriting.
* Draft review links do not allow signing or payouts until you send.
* Archived drafts are removed after the retention period in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
* Drafts are encrypted in your browser before upload. You may need to unlock wallet keys to save.

## How to work with drafts in the app

### Create and save

1. Start **New envelope**.
2. Add recipients and documents, then continue to place fields.
3. Add payouts or extra files if you need them.
4. Click **Save draft**.

### Resume and send

1. Open **Drafts** in the sidebar.
2. Click a draft to continue where you left off.
3. Send from the designer when ready. Filosign marks the draft as sent and closes external review links.

### Share for external review

1. Save the draft first.
2. Click **Share draft** and send the view-only link.

### Who can access drafts

| Permission | Typical access |
|------------|----------------|
| Read | Open and view team drafts |
| Write | Edit and save drafts |

Owners and admins usually have full access. Senders can read and write. Viewers can read only.
