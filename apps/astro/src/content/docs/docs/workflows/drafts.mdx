title: Drafts

**Drafts** let you save an envelope before you send it. Come back later, collaborate with your team, share a view-only copy with external reviewers, and send when you are ready.

## Two ways your work is saved

### While you are editing (browser)

On **New envelope**, Filosign keeps your progress in the browser: recipients, documents, signature fields, payouts, and extra files. You can refresh the page in the same browser without losing that work.

### Saved to your workspace

When you click **Save draft**, Filosign encrypts everything and stores it in your workspace. Teammates with access can open it from **Drafts** in the sidebar.

A saved draft includes recipients, message, field placement, payout settings, and file bundles.

## Create and save

1. Start **New envelope**.
2. Add recipients and documents, then continue to place fields.
3. Add payouts or extra files if you need them.
4. Click **Save draft**.

The first save creates the draft. Later saves update the same draft. If two people edit at once, Filosign warns you instead of silently overwriting.

## Resume a draft

1. Open **Drafts** in the sidebar.
2. Click a draft to continue where you left off.

## Send from a draft

Send the envelope from the designer as usual. When it goes out successfully, Filosign marks the draft as sent and closes external review links for that draft.

## Share for external review

**Share draft** emails external reviewers a view-only link. They can read the document but cannot sign or pay. Save the draft first.

Reviewers open a link like `/draft/review?token=…`. Signing and payouts are not available until you send the envelope.

## Comments

Teammates with access can leave **Comments** on a saved draft while you are still preparing it.

## Delete a draft

Delete from the drafts list to archive it.

## Who can access drafts

Access depends on your workspace role:

| Permission | Typical access |
|------------|----------------|
| Read | Open and view team drafts |
| Write | Edit and save drafts |

Owners and admins usually have full access. Senders can read and write. Viewers can read only.

## Encryption

Drafts are encrypted in your browser before upload. Filosign stores encrypted copies. You may need to unlock your wallet keys to save. See [Security](/security) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for how we protect stored data.

## Delete and retention

Archived drafts are removed after the retention period in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). Workspace processing terms are in the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/data-processing-addendum).
