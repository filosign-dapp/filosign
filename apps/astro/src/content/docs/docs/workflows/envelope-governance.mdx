---
title: Void or change a sent envelope
description: Cancel before completion, replace a signer, or adjust payout packets and gated files after send.
---

## Can I cancel after I hit Send?

**Yes, before everyone required has signed.** Workspace **owners** and **admins** can **void** (recall) the envelope. Senders can too in many cases. You cannot void after the envelope is **fully complete** (or after your minimum signature rule is satisfied).

Voiding:

* Stops outstanding invites.
* Marks the workflow as voided in your records.
* Still lets you download a [proof packet](/docs/proof/completion-packet/) showing what happened.

Signatures that already occurred may still appear in the verification record. See [Verification records](/docs/security/on-chain-record/).

Open **Void envelope** from the document page.

## The wrong person is listed as a signer

If they **have not signed yet**, owners and admins can **amend** the roster: replace them with someone else. New invites go out automatically.

If they **already signed**, you need a different workflow (new envelope). Filosign does not silently swap completed signatures.

## Can I change payout packets or gated files after send?

Often **yes**, on eligible plans:

* **Payout packets**: attach, update, or cancel rules that have not executed yet.
* **Gated files**: attach or cancel packets on Teams Pro before they unlock.

Treasury payouts still need your **team wallet** to approve funds. See [Payout packets](/docs/workflows/payouts/).

## Who is allowed to do this?

| Role | Void / amend | Send envelopes |
|------|:------------:|:--------------:|
| Owner, Admin | Yes | Yes |
| Sender | Send only; cannot override admin void rules | Yes |
| Viewer | No | No |

## Coordinate with your team

Use [Comments](/docs/workflows/comments/) on the envelope to agree on a void or signer change before you act.
