## What does my signer see?

After you **send**, each recipient opens a familiar signing page:

1. Accept access to electronic records (if prompted)
2. Read the document
3. Fill assigned fields (signature, date, text, and so on)
4. Confirm signing in the app

Filosign records each step for your [proof export](/docs/proof/completion-packet/). Signers do not need special software.

## Can everyone sign at once?

**Yes, by default.** All signers can open and sign in parallel, like most e-sign tools.

## I need people to sign in order

**Teams Pro** supports **turn order**: signers must sign in the sequence you set (e.g. employee, then manager, then HR).

While composing, open **Signing order** and arrange recipients.

## I only need some signatures, not all

**Teams Pro** supports **minimum signatures**: set how many signers must finish for the envelope to count as complete (for example, 2 of 5 board members).

Enable **Minimum signatures** while composing.

Turn order and minimum signatures affect when [payout packets](/docs/workflows/payouts/) and [gated file release](/docs/workflows/attached-files/) release.

## Recipients without an account yet

Some people receive an invite before they create a Filosign account. They use a **one-time recovery phrase** from the invite to open the document, then complete the same signing steps.

## Viewers

**Viewers** can read but do not sign. Their access is still private and logged in proof exports.

## What happens when signing finishes?

- Status becomes **completed**
- Payouts and locked files [release automatically](/docs/workflows/) when your rules match
- **Proof downloads** unlock

## Problems while signing?

Search [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) for the exact message (for example, waiting for another signer, or open the document before signing).
