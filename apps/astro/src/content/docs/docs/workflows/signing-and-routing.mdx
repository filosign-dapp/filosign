title: Signing and routing

After you **send**, each recipient opens a familiar signing page: accept electronic records (if prompted), read the document, fill assigned fields, and confirm signing in the app. Filosign records each step for your [proof export](/docs/proof/completion-packet/). Signers do not need special software.

## When to use this

* You are setting up an envelope and need to choose parallel vs sequential signing
* You need **minimum signatures** (for example, 2 of 5 board members) on Teams Pro
* A recipient does not have a Filosign account yet
* You want to understand how routing affects payout and gated-file release

## How it works

By default, all signers can open and sign in **parallel**, like most e-sign tools.

**Teams Pro** adds:

* **Turn order**: signers must sign in the sequence you set (for example, employee, then manager, then HR)
* **Minimum signatures**: set how many signers must finish for the envelope to count as complete

Turn order and minimum signatures affect when [payout packets](/docs/workflows/payouts/) and [gated file release](/docs/workflows/attached-files/) release.

**Viewers** can read but do not sign. Their access is still private and logged in proof exports.

Some people receive an invite before they create a Filosign account. They use a **one-time recovery phrase** from the invite to open the document, then complete the same signing steps.

When signing finishes:

* Status becomes **completed**
* Payouts and locked files [release automatically](/docs/workflows/) when your rules match
* **Proof downloads** unlock

## Example workflow

A company sends an employment agreement to an employee, manager, and HR. On Teams Pro, they enable **Signing order** so the employee signs first, then the manager, then HR. The attached payout packet uses **Pay out when: Everyone required has signed**, which fires after HR completes the sequence.

## What to watch out for

* Minimum signatures and turn order change what "complete" means for release conditions.
* Recipients without accounts need their one-time phrase from the invite.
* Signing problems often mean waiting for another signer or opening the document before signing. Search [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) for the exact message.

## How to set routing in the app

1. While composing, add recipients and assign signer vs viewer roles.
2. Open **Signing order** to arrange turn sequence (Teams Pro).
3. Enable **Minimum signatures** and enter how many signers must finish (Teams Pro).
4. Place fields for each signer, then send.

Signers use the [signature library](/docs/proof/signature-library/) to reuse saved signature styles when fields are assigned to them.
