**Templates** are shared starting documents for your workspace. Use them when your team sends the same agreement often and does not want to re-upload the PDF every time.

## When to use this

* Your team sends the same NDA, SOW, or policy PDF repeatedly
* You want a library PDF without rebuilding the full envelope each time
* You need a faster starting point before adding recipients and fields

## How it works

A template stores:

* A name (for example, **Standard NDA**)
* The PDF, encrypted for your workspace

Templates save the **PDF only** today. **Placed signature fields are not copied yet.** After **Use template**, you still add fields on the designer. If you need the same field layout every time, keep using a saved draft with fields already placed until template fields ship.

| | Draft | Template |
|---|-------|----------|
| Best for | Work in progress | Starting the same PDF again |
| Holds | Full envelope setup | PDF only |
| Lifecycle | Until sent or deleted | Stays in the library |

## Example workflow

A sales team saves their standard mutual NDA as a template. Each new deal starts from **Use template**, then they add the prospect as a recipient, place signature fields, and send without re-uploading the PDF.

## What to watch out for

* Templates stay inside your workspace. They are not public links.
* Deleting a template removes it from the library. Envelopes you already sent from that template are not affected.
* You need a **saved draft** before creating a template from the designer.

## How to use templates in the app

### Create a template

You need a **saved draft** first.

**From the Templates page:**

1. Click **New Template**.
2. Pick a saved draft and enter a name.
3. Confirm.

**From the designer:**

1. Open a saved draft.
2. Click **Save as Template** and enter a name.

### Use a template

1. On **Templates**, click **Use template**.
2. Filosign starts a new envelope with that PDF.
3. Add recipients, place fields, attach payouts or extra files, then send or save as a draft.

### Who can do what

| Role | Typical access |
|------|----------------|
| Owner / admin | Create and delete templates |
| Sender / viewer | Start new envelopes from templates |
