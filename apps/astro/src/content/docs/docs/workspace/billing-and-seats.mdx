---
title: Billing and seats
description: How Filosign plans, trials, seats, and monthly document quotas work for Solo and team workspaces.
---

Workspace **billing** lives under **Workspace settings**. The active plan controls monthly send limits, signer caps, and which collaboration features your org can use.

## When to use this

* You are choosing Solo vs Teams vs Teams Pro
* You need to add or remove seats on a team plan
* You want to understand pooled monthly document quotas
* You are adding archival storage separately from your sending plan

## How it works

| Plan | Best for | Documents / month | Signers / envelope |
|------|----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Solo** | Individual operators | 10 (account) | 3 |
| **Teams** | Shared workflows | 15 per seat (pooled) | 10 |
| **Teams Pro** | Advanced routing and payouts | 25 per seat (pooled) | 15 |
| **Enterprise** | Custom contracts | By agreement | By agreement |

Paid plans include a **7-day free trial** when you subscribe through the app. Team document quota is **pooled**: 15 or 25 documents per seat per month, shared across the workspace. Solo quota is per account, not per seat.

**Seat quota allocation** (splitting monthly sends per member) is on the [roadmap](/docs/plans/roadmap/) and is not available in the app yet.

Archival storage is a **separate yearly subscription**. You do not need Teams Pro for storage alone.

## Example workflow

A ten-person team subscribes to Teams with eight seats. They share a pooled quota of 120 documents per month (15 × 8) and upgrade to Teams Pro when they need signing order and multi-recipient payout rules.

## What to watch out for

* Each active member and pending invite counts toward your seat minimum.
* Downgrading may remove access to team features (shared templates, team drafts, comments) when the new plan does not include them.
* Quotas reset each calendar month.

## How to manage billing in the app

From **Workspace settings** → **Billing** you can:

* Start a trial or subscribe to Solo, Teams, or Teams Pro
* Switch between Teams and Teams Pro
* Add or remove seats
* Open the billing portal to update payment method or cancel
