---
title: Billing and seats
description: How Filosign plans, trials, seats, and monthly document quotas work for Solo and team workspaces.
---

Workspace **billing** lives under **Workspace settings**. The active plan controls monthly send limits, signer caps, and which collaboration features your org can use.

## Plans

| Plan | Best for | Documents / month | Signers / envelope |
|------|----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Solo** | Individual operators | 10 (account) | 3 |
| **Teams** | Shared workflows | 15 per seat (pooled) | 10 |
| **Teams Pro** | Advanced routing and payouts | 25 per seat (pooled) | 15 |
| **Enterprise** | Custom contracts | By agreement | By agreement |

Paid plans include a **7-day free trial** when you subscribe through the app. See the full feature list on [Plans and limits](/docs/plans/) and the [Pricing](/pricing) page.

## Seats (Teams and Teams Pro)

You pay per **seat**. Each active member and pending invite counts toward your seat minimum. Owners and admins can add or remove seats in **Workspace settings** → **Billing**.

Team document quota is **pooled**: 15 or 25 documents per seat per month, shared across the workspace. Solo quota is per account, not per seat.

**Seat quota allocation** (splitting monthly sends per member) is on the [roadmap](/docs/plans/roadmap/) and is not available in the app yet.

## Change plans

From **Billing** you can:

- Start a trial or subscribe to Solo, Teams, or Teams Pro
- Switch between Teams and Teams Pro
- Open the billing portal to update payment method or cancel

Downgrading may remove access to team features (shared templates, team drafts, comments) when the new plan does not include them.

## Archival storage (optional)

Paid workspaces can add a **separate yearly archival subscription** to keep signed documents for 1, 3, or 5 years. You do not need Teams Pro for storage alone. See [Keeping documents long term](/docs/storage/how-retention-works/).
