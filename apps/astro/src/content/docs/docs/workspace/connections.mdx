**Connections** is where your workspace keeps a list of people you send documents to often. Add recipients by email before you compose, then add them to envelopes like any other contact.

## When to use this

* You send to the same clients, vendors, or investors repeatedly
* You want a simple recipient list before starting a new envelope
* You are reducing signing spam by inviting only people you trust

## How it works

Today, Connections provides:

* A list of **email invites** your workspace has sent
* A simple place to grow your recipient list before you compose

Filosign is also building stronger **recipient controls** so people can approve which senders may reach them. That approval flow is **not fully live yet**. Today, you reduce unwanted signing by only adding recipients you trust and using private, invite-only envelopes.

| Role | Can invite by email |
|------|---------------------|
| Owner, Admin, Sender | Yes |
| Viewer | No |

## Example workflow

A sales team adds five prospect emails to Connections during onboarding. When a new NDA is ready, the sender picks those contacts from the list instead of retyping addresses.

## What to watch out for

* Connections does not stop all unwanted signing by itself.
* Full recipient-side sender approval is still rolling out. We will update this page when it ships broadly.
* People still need an account and an invite to participate in encrypted envelopes.

## How to use Connections in the app

1. Open **Connections** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add recipient**, enter their email, and send the invite.
3. When they join Filosign, add them to envelopes while composing.
