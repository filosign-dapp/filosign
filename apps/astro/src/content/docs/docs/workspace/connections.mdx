## How do I add someone I send documents to often?

Open **Connections** in the sidebar. Click **Add recipient**, enter their email, and send the invite.

When they join Filosign, add them to envelopes like any other contact.

## What Connections do today

- A list of **email invites** your workspace has sent
- A simple place to grow your recipient list before you compose

## Will this stop signing spam?

**Partially.** People need an account and an invite to participate in your encrypted envelopes. Filosign is also building stronger **recipient controls** so people can approve which senders may reach them. That approval flow is **not fully live yet**.

Today, you reduce unwanted signing by:

- Only adding recipients you trust
- Using private, invite-only envelopes

We will update this page when recipient-side sender approval ships broadly.

## Who can invite?

| Role | Can invite by email |
|------|---------------------|
| Owner, Admin, Sender | Yes |
| Viewer | No |
