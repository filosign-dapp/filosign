## What is a workspace?

A **workspace** is your team's home in Filosign: billing, members, shared drafts, and templates. Solo users have a personal workspace. Growing teams invite colleagues into the same one.

## Common questions

### Where do I manage billing?

**Workspace settings** → **Billing**. Change plan, seats, or open the payment portal.

### How do teammates share work?

On **Teams** and above: shared **Drafts**, **Templates**, and envelopes visible on **Home**. See [Members and roles](/docs/workspace/members-and-roles/).

### Where do I buy long-term storage?

**Workspace settings** → **Archival**. That is a **separate yearly subscription** for keeping signed documents 1, 3, or 5 years. See [Keeping documents long term](/docs/storage/how-retention-works/).

### How do I switch workspaces?

Use the workspace menu in the sidebar footer. You can create another workspace on **Teams** or **Teams Pro** if you own a paid org.

## What's in workspace settings

| Section | Solves |
|---------|--------|
| **Details** | Rename the workspace |
| **Team members** | Invite people, set roles |
| **Billing** | Plan and seats |
| **Archival** | Multi-year document retention |
| **Treasury wallet** | Team wallet for USDC payouts |
| **Payout access** | Request approval to attach payouts |

## Solo vs Teams (quick pick)

| Need | Plan |
|------|------|
| Private signing and proof exports | **Solo** |
| Shared templates, team drafts, comments | **Teams** |
| Signing order, quorum, advanced payouts | **Teams Pro** |

[Plans and limits](/docs/plans/) has the full comparison.
