A **workspace** is your team's home in Filosign: billing, members, shared drafts, and templates. Solo users have a personal workspace. Growing teams invite colleagues into the same one.

## When to use this

* You are onboarding teammates and need to know where billing and roles live
* You want long-term document retention separate from your sending plan
* You need a team wallet for USDC payout packets
* You are choosing between Solo, Teams, and Teams Pro

## How it works

| Section | Solves |
|---------|--------|
| **Details** | Rename the workspace |
| **Team members** | Invite people, set roles |
| **Billing** | Plan and seats |
| **Archival** | Multi-year document retention |
| **Treasury wallet** | Team wallet for USDC payouts |
| **Payout access** | Request approval to attach payouts |

On **Teams** and above: shared **Drafts**, **Templates**, and envelopes visible on **Home**.

| Need | Plan |
|------|------|
| Private signing and proof exports | **Solo** |
| Shared templates, team drafts, comments | **Teams** |
| Signing order, quorum, advanced payouts | **Teams Pro** |

## Example workflow

A five-person startup upgrades from Solo to Teams. The admin invites colleagues, enables shared drafts and templates, and links a treasury wallet for contractor payout packets. Archival is added separately for three-year retention.

## What to watch out for

* Your **personal workspace** (the first one you create) can stay on **Free** or upgrade to **Solo** anytime.
* **Each additional workspace** needs its own **Teams** or **Teams Pro** subscription at creation. Complete checkout, then name the workspace.
* Archival is a **separate yearly subscription**, not bundled into Teams Pro.
* Payout packets need [payout access](/docs/workspace/payout-access/) approval plus wallet setup.

## Creating another workspace

1. Open the workspace menu in the sidebar footer and choose **Create workspace** (or use **New workspace** in Workspace settings).
2. If you already own a workspace, pick a plan and finish checkout.
3. After checkout, name the workspace and confirm create. Billing is attached to that workspace only.

## How to manage your workspace in the app

1. Open **Workspace settings** from the sidebar footer.
2. Use **Team members** to invite people and set roles.
3. Use **Billing** to change plan or seats.
4. Use **Archival** to buy multi-year retention.
5. Use **Treasury wallet** and **Payout attachment access** before team payouts.

Use the workspace menu in the sidebar footer to switch workspaces.
