---
title: Treasury wallet
description: Link a team treasury wallet (often a Safe) so payout rules can pay from shared funds.
---

A **treasury wallet** is an external wallet your workspace links for **USDC payout packets**. It is often a multisig Safe or another treasury address your finance team controls.

Filosign does not hold payout funds. The linked wallet must approve payment when a payout runs.

## When you need it

Link a treasury wallet when:

- Payouts should come from team funds, not an individual sender wallet
- Finance requires a multisig to approve outgoing USDC

Individual senders can still attach payouts from their own wallet without linking a treasury.

## Link a wallet

1. Open **Workspace settings** → **Workspace treasury**.
2. Connect the wallet you want to link (must be able to sign).
3. Confirm the link in the app.

The linked address appears in payout compose options as the **organization payout wallet**.

## Payout rules and the treasury

When you attach a payout leg to the org wallet:

- The **treasury wallet** must approve USDC spending for Filosign to execute the transfer
- Recipients must still be on the envelope (or the linked org wallet) per payout policy

Treasury payouts are separate from **payout attachment access**, the manual review step Filosign may require before your workspace can attach payouts at all. See [Payout access](/docs/workspace/payout-access/).

## Governance note

Workspace owners and admins can void envelopes and amend signers. Treasury payouts still require the **treasury wallet itself** to sign as payer when funds move.
