## Why you see this

Your browser could not complete a request to Filosign. That is usually a network, VPN, or temporary outage issue - not a problem with your document.

## What to do

1. Check that you are connected to the internet.
2. Retry the action.
3. Try another network or disable VPN if you use one.

## Still stuck?

Wait a few minutes and try again. Contact support if only Filosign fails while other sites work.
