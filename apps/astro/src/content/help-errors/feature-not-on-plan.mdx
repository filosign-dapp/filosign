## Why you see this

The action uses a workspace capability that your current plan does not include (checked server-side via entitlements).

## What to do

1. In the sidebar, open **Account → Workspace**.
2. Scroll to **Billing & Plans** and compare available plans.
3. Upgrade if needed, then retry the action.

## Still stuck?

After upgrading, refresh the app or sign out and back in. Contact support if billing shows the right plan but the feature stays locked.
