---
code: ENTITLEMENT.LIMIT_EXCEEDED
title: Over the plan limit for this action
description: This action goes past a fixed limit on your current plan (not a metered quota).
---

## Why you see this

Your plan has a **fixed cap** on this action (for example, maximum recipients per send). That is different from a metered quota that resets each billing period.

## What to do

1. Reduce what you are trying to do (for example, fewer recipients on the envelope).
2. Open **Account → Workspace → Billing & Plans** to review limits or upgrade.

## Still stuck?

Split the work across multiple sends if needed. Contact support if you need a limit raised on a paid plan.
