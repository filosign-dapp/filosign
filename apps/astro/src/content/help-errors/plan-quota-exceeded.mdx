code: ENTITLEMENT.QUOTA_EXCEEDED
title: Plan usage limit reached
description: You have reached a metered limit for this billing period.
---

## Why you see this

Your workspace hit a **metered** limit that resets each billing period. The error toast shows how much you have used versus your limit; this help page does not repeat those numbers.

## What to do

1. Open **Account → Workspace** and review **Billing & Plans**.
2. Wait for the quota to reset, or upgrade to a higher plan.

## Still stuck?

Confirm you are in the correct workspace. Contact support if usage on the billing page looks lower than the error suggests.
