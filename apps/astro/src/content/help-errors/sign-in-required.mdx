code: AUTH.UNAUTHORIZED
title: Sign in required
description: Your session expired or you are not signed in.
---

## Why you see this

Filosign could not verify an active session for this action. That can happen after timeout, a new browser, or signing out on another tab.

## What to do

1. Sign in again with your wallet.
2. Complete wallet unlock if prompted.
3. Return to the page you were using.

## Still stuck?

Clear site data only as a last resort (you may need to unlock again). Contact support if sign-in succeeds but protected pages still fail.
