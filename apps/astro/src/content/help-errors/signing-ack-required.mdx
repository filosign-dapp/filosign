## Why you see this

Before decrypting the document, Filosign requires an on-chain **Accept file** acknowledgement. Until that is stored, you cannot open the PDF or sign.

## What to do

1. On the sign page, read the acceptance text (for example, that you agree to review the document electronically).
2. Tap **Accept file** and wait for it to finish.
3. After the PDF loads, complete your fields and tap **Sign**.

## Still stuck?

If **Accept file** fails, sign out and back in, then open the same invite link again.
