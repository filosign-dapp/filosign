## Why you see this

Filosign already recorded your signature for this document. Signing again is not needed.

## What to do

1. Open your dashboard and download the completed copy if you need one.
2. Wait for other signers if the envelope is still in progress.

## Still stuck?

If you did not sign yet, refresh the page or sign out and back in with the same email the sender used. Contact support with the document link if your status looks wrong.
