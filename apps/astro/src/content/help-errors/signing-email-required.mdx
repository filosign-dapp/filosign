## Why you see this

Fields on this document are assigned by email. Filosign matches your **primary profile email** to those assignments before you can sign.

## What to do

1. In the sidebar, open **Account → Profile**.
2. Add or verify your **primary email** (the same address the sender used for you).
3. Return to the sign page and tap **Sign** again.

## Still stuck?

Ask the sender which email they used on the document. Update your profile to match exactly.
