---
code: SIGNING.FIELD_AUTO_REQUIRED
title: Auto-fill value required
description: Date, name, or email fields need a value before signing.
---

## Why you see this

You are signing a document that has auto-fill fields (such as date, name, or email) assigned to you, but one or more of these fields do not have a value applied.

## What to do

1. Find any auto-fill fields on the document.
2. Tap each auto-fill field to populate it with your profile details (current date, name, or email address).
3. Confirm all values are correct, then tap **Sign** again.
