---
code: SIGNING.FIELD_COMPLETION_MISSING
title: Field value missing
description: A completed field is missing its applied value.
---

## Why you see this

You are attempting to sign a document but one of the fields you completed does not have its value correctly applied or recorded.

## What to do

1. Locate the field that is missing a value on the document.
2. Tap the field again to apply your signature, initial, or text value.
3. Once all fields show as completed, tap **Sign** again.
