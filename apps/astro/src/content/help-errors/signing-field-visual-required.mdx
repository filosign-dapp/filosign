title: Signature image required
description: Signature and initial fields need a visual applied.
---

## Why you see this

You are trying to sign a document that has signature or initial fields assigned to you, but you haven't selected or created a visual signature to apply to them.

## What to do

1. Create a signature in your library if you have not already (go to **Account** → **Signature Library**).
2. Tap each signature/initial field on the document to apply your default signature.
3. Tap **Sign** again.
