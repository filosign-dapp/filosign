## Why you see this

Your account opened this document, but the sender did not add you as a required signer. You may have been invited only to view or receive a copy.

## What to do

1. Confirm you used the invite link from the sender (check the email address).
2. If you expected to sign, ask the sender to add you as a signer and resend the invite.

## Still stuck?

You can still download or view completed documents from your dashboard when you have access. Contact support if you believe your role is wrong.
