## Why you see this

The sender placed signature or other fields on the PDF for you. Filosign checks that every **required** field is marked complete before it accepts your signature.

## What to do

1. On the sign page, tap each required field on the PDF (short labels show **Req** until done, then **Done**).
2. When all required fields are complete, tap **Sign**.
3. In the **Confirm signing intent** dialog, read the text and confirm.

## Still stuck?

Zoom in on each page - small fields are easy to miss. The **Sign** button stays disabled until the document is loaded and required fields are done.
