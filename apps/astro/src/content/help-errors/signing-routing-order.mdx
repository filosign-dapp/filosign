## Why you see this

The sender enabled **sequential signing order**. The app compares your email to earlier signers on the envelope registry and blocks signing until they finish. On the sign page, the **Signing progress** section explains who is next.

## What to do

1. Open the sign page and read **Signing progress** (for example, “You'll sign after everyone ahead of you in the signing order”).
2. Wait until earlier signers complete.
3. Return to the same link and tap **Sign** when the **Sign** button is enabled.

## Still stuck?

Filosign does not send a separate “your turn” email for this check - the progress banner and an enabled **Sign** button are your signals. Ask the sender to confirm signing order if you believe it is already your turn.
