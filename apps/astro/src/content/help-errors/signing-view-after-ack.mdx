code: SIGNING.VIEW_AFTER_ACK
title: Open the document after accepting
description: Filosign needs a view recorded after you accept the file, before signing.
---

## Why you see this

Signing order checks require **accept file** first, then a **document view** (when the file loads), then **sign**. This error means the view timestamp is earlier than your acceptance—usually the document had not finished loading after you accepted.

## What to do

1. Tap **Accept file** if you have not already.
2. Wait until the PDF fully loads on the sign page.
3. Tap **Sign** again and confirm in the dialog.

## Still stuck?

Refresh the sign page, accept the file again, wait for the PDF, then sign. Contact support if the error persists after a normal load.
