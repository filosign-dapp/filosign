## Why you see this

Filosign records a **document view** when the file finishes decrypting and loading on the sign page - not when you scroll page by page. Signing is blocked until that view exists.

## What to do

1. Tap **Accept file** if you have not already.
2. Unlock your wallet or enter your recovery phrase if prompted.
3. Wait until the PDF appears on the sign page.
4. Tap **Sign** again.

## Still stuck?

Use **Retry** if the canvas shows a load error. Refresh the page and repeat Accept file → wait for the PDF → Sign.
