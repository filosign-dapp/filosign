## Why you see this

Filosign could not load the signing keys needed for decrypt or sign. That usually means the session is incomplete or wallet setup was interrupted.

## What to do

1. Sign out and sign in again.
2. Complete wallet setup or recovery if the app prompts you.
3. Unlock your wallet or enter your recovery phrase when asked, then retry.

## Still stuck?

Use the same browser profile where you originally set up Filosign. Contact support if onboarding or recovery loops without finishing.
