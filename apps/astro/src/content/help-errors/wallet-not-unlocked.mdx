## Why you see this

Decrypting and signing need your Filosign encryption keys. The app tries wallet unlock first; if that fails, it shows a **recovery phrase** gate.

## What to do

1. Complete the unlock or recovery prompt on screen (24-word recovery phrase if shown).
2. Return to the sign page and wait for the document to load.
3. Retry the action.

## Still stuck?

Sign out, sign in again, and unlock when prompted. Contact support if recovery does not restore access.
