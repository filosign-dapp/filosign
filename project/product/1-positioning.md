# Filosign Positioning & Strategy

Source of truth for how to explain Filosign.

## Core Thesis

Filosign is **private agreement workflow software**.

For crypto teams, signed agreements can also trigger **non-custodial USDC payouts**.

The product should feel like normal e-signing. The blockchain layer should be invisible unless the user needs proof, verification, or settlement.

## One-Liners

Use these:

- Private e-signing with instant stablecoin payouts.
- Secure agreement workflows for internet-native teams.
- Signed agreements that can trigger USDC payouts.
- Private documents, verifiable signatures, executable payouts.

Avoid these as primary one-liners:

- Blockchain e-signatures.
- Post-quantum document signing.
- Decentralized DocuSign.
- FVM-powered legal infrastructure.

## Messaging Hierarchy

1. **Broad product:** encrypted, verifiable document workflows.
2. **Initial wedge:** signed agreement -> USDC payout.
3. **Expansion:** fiat/on-ramps, embedded signing, API workflows, enterprise compliance.

Lead with settlement for crypto accelerators and Web3 buyers. Lead with secure agreement workflow for broader business buyers.

## The Problem

Teams sign agreements in one place and execute the business action somewhere else.

For Web3 teams, this often means:

- signed grant agreement in one tool
- invoice or tax form in another tool
- payment approval in a multisig
- USDC transfer in a wallet
- audit trail assembled later by hand

This is slow, messy, and hard to verify.

## The Insight

For many agreements, **signature is not the final state**.

The final state is:

- the grant is approved
- the contractor is paid
- the milestone is accepted
- the contributor payout is released
- the business commitment is executed

Filosign makes the signed agreement the condition for the next action.

## What Filosign Is

Filosign lets teams:

- send encrypted documents
- invite signers and viewers
- collect verifiable signatures
- export proof/compliance packets
- optionally attach non-custodial USDC payout rules
- settle payouts after signing conditions are met

## What Filosign Is Not

Filosign is not:

- a generic DocuSign clone
- a custodial escrow provider
- a money transmitter
- a QES/eIDAS-qualified signature provider today
- an enterprise compliance suite today
- a product that requires users to understand crypto

## Competitor Frame

DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, Documenso, DocuSeal, and OpenSign treat signing as the end of a document workflow.

Filosign treats signing as a business state transition.

Traditional tools prove someone clicked sign. Filosign proves who signed, what workflow was completed, and can let the related payout execute without Filosign custodying funds.

## Best Pitch

Filosign is private e-signing with instant stablecoin payouts. It feels like a normal e-signature product, but gives teams encrypted documents, independently verifiable signatures, and non-custodial USDC settlement when signing conditions are met.

We are starting with Web3 teams because they already use stablecoins and feel the signing-to-payment pain first. The broader product is secure agreement workflow software for internet-native work.

## Use This Language

- private agreement workflow
- encrypted documents
- verifiable signatures
- proof packet
- non-custodial settlement
- signed agreement becomes the payment condition
- stablecoin payout after signature
- invisible blockchain infrastructure
- Web2-native user experience

## Avoid This Language

- escrow, unless clearly qualified as non-custodial
- guaranteed legal compliance
- qualified electronic signature
- money transmission
- blockchain-powered e-signature as the main pitch
- post-quantum as the first selling point
- “permanent proof” without explaining why it matters
