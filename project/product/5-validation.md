## Customer-Pain Anecdote

During the Filecoin Alpha Cohort, Sarah needed to send a $3,000 payment and request a W-8BEN.

The current flow required:

- sending the form separately
- waiting for completion
- then wiring money to a bank account

With Filosign, the sender could send the form, the recipient could complete and sign it, and a USDC payout could release after signature.

Use this as the clearest narrative for contractor/tax-form/payment workflows.

## Safe Compliance Claims

Use:

- designed to support strong evidence of signer intent, timestamping, document integrity, and auditability
- client-side encryption means Filosign should not need plaintext document access
- non-custodial settlement means users approve exact payout rules and Filosign does not custody funds
- QES/eIDAS-qualified signatures are future/compliance-driven, not current claims

Avoid:

- legally binding in every jurisdiction
- QES-compliant
- money transmitter
- escrow provider
- enterprise compliant
- zero-risk
- guaranteed legal enforceability
