# Filosign Lead Generation Agent Brief

Use this brief to run refined lead-hunt sprints for Filosign.

## Product Narrative

Filosign is private agreement workflow software.

Use this one-liner:

> Filosign helps teams send private agreements, collect signatures, export proof, and unlock payouts or files when signing conditions are met.

Do not pitch Filosign as:

- a blockchain e-signature product
- a DocuSign clone
- an escrow product
- a crypto wallet product
- a legal compliance replacement

The human pain:

> The signed PDF is not the end. Teams still need to verify who signed, release files, approve payouts, and prove what happened later.

The product outcome:

> One private workflow for the agreement, the proof, the payout packet, and the secure handoff.

## Best First Customers

Prioritize teams where signing unlocks a real next step.

### Tier 1: Web3 Grants And Ecosystem Programs

Best fit because they already pay global contributors, often in stablecoins, and need milestone paperwork before payout.

Look for:

- grant programs
- ecosystem funds
- builder programs
- public goods funding
- accelerator or residency programs
- hackathon prize operations
- milestone-based contributor payouts

Relevant titles:

- Ecosystem Lead
- Grants Lead
- Developer Relations Lead
- Program Manager
- Operations Lead
- Community Lead
- Treasury Operations

Pain signals:

- “milestone”
- “grant agreement”
- “KYC / tax form”
- “invoice”
- “USDC payout”
- “contributors”
- “bounties”
- “manual review”
- “proof of work”
- “payment delays”

### Tier 2: Bounty, Hackathon, And Contributor Platforms

Best fit when many recipients must submit paperwork before payout.

Look for:

- hackathon platforms
- bounty platforms
- ecosystem challenge platforms
- contributor reward platforms
- grant administration tools

Relevant titles:

- Founder
- Head of Operations
- Partnerships
- Program Operations
- Community Operations

Pain signals:

- many winners or contributors
- manual payout batches
- forms before payouts
- public payout announcements
- cross-border recipients
- stablecoin payout support

### Tier 3: Crypto Payroll, Contractor, And Stablecoin Payment Tools

Often better as partners or channels than direct customers.

Look for:

- crypto payroll tools
- contractor payout platforms
- stablecoin invoice tools
- DAO treasury tooling
- multisig operations tooling

Classify carefully:

- Customer if they run their own contractor agreements.
- Partner if they already own payouts but lack document workflows.
- Channel if they serve the exact customer Filosign wants.
- Ignore if they only do consumer remittance or trading.

### Tier 4: Agencies, Dev Studios, And AI / Deep-Tech Startups

Good secondary wedge for gated file handoff.

Look for:

- dev studios delivering source code
- AI startups sharing sensitive data or IP documents
- agencies sending final deliverables after approval
- M&A-adjacent or diligence workflows

This is not the first outbound priority unless there is a strong trigger.

## Lead Scoring

Score each lead from 0 to 10.

Use this rubric:

- +3 if they pay contributors, grantees, winners, contractors, or vendors
- +2 if a document, form, agreement, invoice, or milestone must happen before payout
- +2 if they already use USDC, stablecoins, crypto wallets, or multisig tools
- +1 if they operate cross-border
- +1 if they mention grants, bounties, hackathons, contributor programs, or ecosystem ops
- +1 if there is a named operator or decision-maker
- -3 if the company is only consumer payments, trading, exchange, or wallet infrastructure
- -2 if there is no clear agreement or proof workflow
- -2 if the only reason for inclusion is “they are Web3”

Priority:

- 8-10: High-priority lead
- 6-7: Worth outbound
- 4-5: Keep for later
- 0-3: Ignore

## Best First Ask

Do not ask for a demo first.

Use one of these:

- “How do you handle the signed paperwork before payouts today?”
- “What has to be completed before a grant or bounty payout is released?”
- “Who verifies the agreement, milestone, or tax form before payment?”
- “Where does this workflow break today: signing, proof, file handoff, or payout?”

## Outreach Positioning

Use this short pitch:

> Filosign is a private agreement workflow for teams whose signed documents need to unlock the next step. You can collect signatures, export proof, and optionally attach USDC payout packets or gated files that release only when signing conditions are met.

Use this for grants:

> Filosign helps grant teams collect milestone paperwork and keep a clean proof trail before payout. If the team pays in USDC, an approved payout packet can be attached to the agreement so the payment step is no longer a separate manual chase.

Use this for agencies / dev studios:

> Filosign lets teams attach sensitive deliverables to the agreement and unlock them only after the required sign-off is complete.

Use this for partners:

> Filosign can sit before your payout rail: collect the agreement, confirm the signing condition, export proof, then hand off to the payout workflow.

## What Not To Say

Never say:

- “DocuSign is insecure.”
- “Legally binding everywhere.”
- “Guaranteed payment.”
- “Escrow.”
- “Filosign holds funds.”
- “No trust needed.”
- “Blockchain e-signature platform.”
- “Permanent storage forever.”
- “Mainnet traction” unless verified.

Safer language:

- “private documents”
- “proof packets”
- “payout packets”
- “gated file release”
- “approved payouts”
- “signing conditions”
- “Filosign does not hold payout funds”
- “designed to support evidence of what happened”

## Lead Hunt Sprints

### Sprint 1: Filecoin And PL Network

Goal: 30 leads.

Find teams in the Filecoin, Protocol Labs, FIL-B, FilOz, Akindo, DePIN Day, and adjacent builder ecosystems that run grants, bounties, contributors, or partner programs.

Classify:

- direct design partner
- warm intro target
- ecosystem channel
- ignore

### Sprint 2: Web3 Grant Operators

Goal: 50 leads.

Find grants and ecosystem programs across L1s, L2s, DeFi protocols, AI x crypto ecosystems, DePIN ecosystems, and open-source funding programs.

Prioritize programs with public milestone, application, payout, or contributor language.

### Sprint 3: Hackathon And Bounty Operations

Goal: 40 leads.

Find platforms and program teams that manage winners, submissions, prize approvals, bounty completion, and payout workflows.

Prioritize teams with many recipients and public winner payout announcements.

### Sprint 4: Stablecoin Payroll And Contractor Tools

Goal: 35 leads.

Find partner/channel candidates that already serve Web3 teams paying contractors or contributors.

Do not treat every payroll company as a customer. Decide whether they are customer, partner, channel, or ignore.

### Sprint 5: Secure Handoff Workflows

Goal: 30 leads.

Find dev studios, AI startups, and agencies where sensitive files are delivered only after approval or payment.

This sprint is secondary. Only include leads with a clear handoff pain signal.

## Quality Bar

A good lead is not “a famous crypto company.”

A good lead has:

- a signing or approval step
- a payout, file release, or proof step after signing
- a named operator
- a public trigger source
- a plausible first workflow Filosign can pilot

If the agent cannot explain the workflow in one sentence, reject the lead.
