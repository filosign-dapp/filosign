## Sales Philosophy

Early sales is diagnosis, not persuasion.

The goal is to find teams already suffering from signing-plus-payment friction, then learn their exact workflow.

Do not start with:

- “Do you need e-signatures?”
- “Want to try blockchain signing?”
- “Can I demo Filosign?”

Start with:

- “What has to be signed before you release payment?”
- “How do you handle grant or contractor payout approvals today?”
- “Where does the agreement workflow end and payment workflow begin?”

## First 30 Days

### Week 1: Build Lead List

Create 100 targeted leads:

- 30 grant and ecosystem teams
- 25 bounty/hackathon/contributor platforms
- 25 crypto payroll or contractor payment platforms
- 20 DAO ops, treasury, legal, or stablecoin payment partners

Required fields:

- company
- segment
- contact name
- title
- email / X / LinkedIn
- source URL
- trigger signal
- suspected workflow
- personalization note
- priority
- customer / partner / channel classification

### Week 2: Send Small Batches

Send 20-30 high-quality founder emails.

Goal:

- 5-8 replies
- 3-5 discovery calls
- clear objections and workflow notes

### Week 3: Discovery Calls

Do not demo first. Learn the workflow.

Ask:

- What documents must be completed before payout?
- Who signs them?
- Who approves payment?
- Where does payment happen?
- What breaks or gets delayed?
- What proof do you need later?
- Would automatic USDC settlement help, or only proof/workflow?

### Week 4: Convert Pilots

Offer a one-month guided pilot.

Success is not “they liked it.” Success is:

- they give one real workflow
- they agree to test it
- they name the success criteria
- they identify the blocker to paid use

## Objection Handling

### “We do not need automated settlement.”

That is fine. Filosign handles private signing, viewer permissions, and cryptographic proof packets. Settlement is optional.

### “We cannot pay in crypto.”

Use Filosign for document workflow and proof first. Fiat/on-ramp settlement is planned for later.

### “We already use DocuSign.”

DocuSign proves someone clicked a button. Filosign verifies signature identity, client-side encrypts the document, and generates a portable proof packet that connects directly to the business execution.

### “This sounds legally complex.”

Filosign is designed for evidence, document integrity, and cryptographic proof. We do not claim QES or broad legal certification today.

### “Why blockchain?”

Users do not need to care or know. The blockchain layer is used to verify signatures independently and support non-custodial payouts.

## Seniority Targeting (ATL vs BTL)

*   **ATL (Above-the-Line):** Executives, Founders, CFOs. Keep emails to 2-3 sentences. Focus on audit readiness, risk mitigation, and preventing payment leakage (paying before milestones are fully signed).
*   **BTL (Below-the-Line):** DevRel, Program Managers, Operations. Keep emails to 3-4 sentences. Focus on daily workflows, time saved, and eliminating the manual pdf-to-wallet payment chase.

## Agent Instructions

Sales agents should:

- find workflow-triggered leads, not famous companies
- prioritize buyer relevance over company fame
- cite source URLs for every trigger
- classify each lead as customer, partner, channel, or ignore
- write personalization around workflow pain
- avoid fake compliments
- never claim mainnet traction or legal certification
