# Customer & Use-Case Framework

Source of truth for who Filosign should target first. Pilot workflow list: [`../../product/scope/roadmap.md`](../../product/scope/roadmap.md) § Pilot Workflows.

## Primary ICP

Target teams that repeatedly need a document completed before releasing a payment.

Best first segments:

1. **Web3 grant and ecosystem teams**
2. **Bounty, hackathon, and contributor platforms**
3. **Crypto payroll and contractor payment platforms**

These teams already use wallets, stablecoins, global contributors, and operational workflows where agreement + payout are linked.

## Secondary ICP

Pursue after messaging is validated:

- DAO ops and treasury service providers
- stablecoin payment companies
- Web3 legal, tax, and compliance providers
- Filecoin / Protocol Labs ecosystem companies
- crypto startups hiring global contractors

## Use Cases

### Grant Milestone Workflow

User signs a grant agreement, milestone acceptance, or final report. Payment releases after the required signatures or approvals.

Best for:

- foundations
- ecosystem teams
- public goods programs
- protocol grant programs

### Contractor Onboarding Workflow

Contractor signs an agreement, W-8BEN/W-9, invoice, or handover document. Payment releases after document completion.

Best for:

- crypto startups
- payroll platforms
- global contractor teams
- Web3 service providers

### Bounty / Hackathon Payout Workflow

Winner or contributor completes required paperwork. Payout releases after signature or approval.

Best for:

- hackathon platforms
- bounty platforms
- ecosystem quest platforms
- developer communities

### DAO / Treasury Workflow

Contributor, vendor, or service provider signs an agreement. DAO ops or treasury team uses the proof packet for payment approval.

Settlement may be automatic or manual.

### Secure Document Workflow

Encrypted document sending, signing, permissions, and proof without automated payout.

This is useful when the customer likes the document workflow but cannot use settlement yet.

## Buyer Titles

Prioritize:

- Grants Lead
- Ecosystem Lead
- Developer Relations Lead
- Head of Operations
- Finance Lead
- Treasury Lead
- DAO Ops Lead
- Payroll / Compliance Lead
- Founder / COO at small crypto teams

Avoid starting with celebrity founders unless there is a warm path.

## Trigger Signals

Good leads recently:

- launched a grants program
- announced an ecosystem fund
- opened RFPs
- ran a bounty or hackathon
- hired contributors or contractors
- announced stablecoin payments
- posted DAO treasury proposals
- paid retroactive grants
- published contributor program details

## Pain Signals

Look for:

- global contributor payments
- tax forms before payment
- milestone-based grants
- manual invoice collection
- multisig approval delays
- separate agreement and payout tools
- lack of proof around who approved what
- repeated legal/payment operations

## Disqualifiers

Deprioritize:

- generic Web2 SaaS companies
- pure NFT or memecoin communities
- teams with no contributor/payment workflow
- huge enterprises without a direct warm intro
- random famous crypto CEOs
- companies where the only fit is “they sign documents”

## Lead Quality Rubric

High-quality lead:

- signs agreements or forms regularly
- pays external people or teams
- uses stablecoins or crypto rails
- has a clear trigger signal
- has an identifiable ops/grants/finance buyer
- can run a low-risk pilot

Medium-quality lead:

- has document workflows but unclear payout pain
- could be a partner/channel
- needs more discovery

Low-quality lead:

- generic company
- no clear payment workflow
- only famous or impressive
- no reachable buyer

## Best First Lead Categories

Start with:

- Filecoin ecosystem teams
- Web3 grant programs
- crypto payroll / contractor platforms
- bounty and hackathon platforms
- DAO ops providers

Do not broaden to generic e-signature buyers until one wedge converts.
